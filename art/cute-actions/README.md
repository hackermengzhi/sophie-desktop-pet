# Cute life actions

Approved generated sprite strips:

- `backpack-outing.png` — a one-way backpack-ready sequence that holds its
  final “ready to go” pose.
- `affectionate.png` — a looping shy, affectionate expression.
- `joy-jump.png` — a one-way joyful jump that holds its safe grounded pose.

The hatch-pet extraction step produces their approved four-frame rows;
`tools/append_life_action_rows.py` then places those rows in the production
atlas. Row 13 remains reserved for a future delighted expression animation.
